# Income dataset

Earnings by sex and educational attainment for 2021 (source: US Census
Bureau)

## Usage

``` r
data(income)
```

## Format

A data frame with 10 rows and the following variables:

- Education:

  Level of educational attainment

  - `NoHS`: Less than High School Graduate

  - `HS`: High School Graduate (Includes Equivalency)

  - `AD`:Some College or Associate’s Degree

  - `Grad`:Bachelor’s Degree or Greater

- Sex:

  Male or Female

- Earnings:

  Earnings in 2021 dollars
