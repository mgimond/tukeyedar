# Confidence in American institutions survey data

Table 2-12 in *Exploring Data tables, Trends, and Shapes* listing the
percentage of respondents indicating "a great deal" or "quite a lot" of
confidence in American institutions between the years 1973 and 1980.

## Usage

``` r
data(edtts2.12)
```

## Format

A data frame with the following variables:

- institution:

  American institutions

- year:

  Survey year

- perc:

  Percentage of respondents indicating "a great deal" or quite a lot" of
  confidence in American institutions

## References

- Hoaglin, David C. and Mosteller, Frederick and Tukey, John W. (1985).
  Exploring data tables, trends, and shapes. Wiley.
