# Weight Gain in Pigs Data

Table 4-2 in *Exploring Data tables, Trends, and Shapes* is derived from
an experiment conducted at the Iowa Agricultural Experiment Station. It
summarizes the weight gain of male pigs across various combinations of
three dietary supplements

## Usage

``` r
data(edtts4.2)
```

## Format

A data frame with the following variables:

- Methionine:

  Methionine offered at 3 levels: 0%, 0.25% amd 0.50%

- Protein:

  Soybean meal protein offered at 2 levels: 12% and 14%

- Lysine:

  Lysine offered at 4 levels: 0%, 0.05%, 0.1% and 0.15%

- WeightGain:

  Treatment totals of average daily weight gains (measured in units of
  .01 lb) for the combined results for two pigs
