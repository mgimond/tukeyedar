# American Community Survey 2022 Median household income and median rent by county

American Community Survey 2022 Median Household Income (B19013001) and
Median Gross Rent (B25064001) by county. Units are in 2022
inflation-Adjusted Dollars.

## Usage

``` r
incrent22
```

## Format

A data frame with 3222 rows and 5 variables:

- FIPS:

  County/state FIPS code.

- County:

  County name

- State:

  State name.

- Income:

  Median Household Income in 2022 US Dollars.

- Rent:

  Median Gross Rent in 2022 US Dollars.

## Source

https://www.census.gov/
